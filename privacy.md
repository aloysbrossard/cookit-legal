# Privacy Policy for Cookit!

**Last updated: October 3, 2026**

Cookit! ("the app", "we") is a fridge-tracking and recipe app developed by Aloys Brossard, based in France. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

**Contact:** cookit.app.support@protonmail.com

## 1. Information we collect

**Account information.** If you create an account, we collect your email address and, if you sign in with Apple or Google, the name and email those services share with us. You can use Apple's "Hide My Email" option. You can also use the app as a guest without an account.

**Profile information.** The chef name and signature color you choose.

**Content you create.** Fridge items (name, category, expiry date, quantity level), grocery list items, and household activity (who added or changed what). If you create or join a household, other members can see the items and activity in it, along with the chef name and color of whoever added them.

**Recipes and photos.** Saved recipes, including cover photos, are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers.

**Usage and purchase information.** We count how often you use limited features (AI Chef, recipe import, barcode scanner) so the free and Pro limits work. Subscription status is handled by Apple and RevenueCat. We do not see or store your payment card details.

**Barcodes.** When you scan a product, the barcode number is sent to the Open Food Facts database to look up the product name.

**Device information.** The app does not use advertising identifiers and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## 2. How we use information

- To provide the app: sign you in, sync your fridge and grocery lists, and share them with your household.
- To generate recipes and import recipes using AI (see section 3).
- To apply feature limits and manage your Pro subscription.
- To send login codes by email, and local reminders about food that is about to expire (reminders are created on your device).
- To fix bugs and keep the app secure.

We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for advertising.

## 3. AI features and third parties

When you use **AI Chef**, the ingredient names you select, your servings, your kitchen equipment choices, and your app language are sent to **OpenAI** to generate recipes. When you use **recipe import**, the text you paste or the photo you choose is sent to OpenAI to extract a recipe. We do not send your name or email with these requests. OpenAI processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy.

We use these service providers to run the app:

| Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Supabase | Account sign-in, database and file storage for signed-in users |
| OpenAI | AI recipe generation and import |
| RevenueCat | Subscription status and purchase management |
| Apple | Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases, TestFlight |
| Google | Sign in with Google |
| Resend | Sending login-code emails |
| Open Food Facts | Looking up product names from barcodes |

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where this happens, we rely on the safeguards those providers offer, such as standard contractual clauses.

## 4. Where your data is stored

- **Signed-in users:** fridge items, grocery items, activity, and profile data are stored on our servers (Supabase) and also cached on your device.
- **Guests:** data stays on your device only.
- **Recipes:** stored on your device only.

If you delete the app without an account, your guest data is lost.

## 5. Your choices and rights

**Delete your account.** Go to **Settings → Delete Account** in the app. This permanently deletes your account and the data we hold about you. If you share a household, the shared items stay with the other members, but your name is removed from them. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke the connection with your Apple ID. Recipes saved on your device are removed too.

**Cancel your subscription.** Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Manage subscriptions at https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions or in your Apple ID settings.

**Other rights.** Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL). To use these rights, email us at the address above.

**Permissions.** The app asks for access to your camera (to scan barcodes), your photo library (to choose recipe photos), and notifications (for expiry reminders). You can change these in your phone's Settings at any time.

## 6. Data retention

We keep your account data until you delete your account. After deletion, your data is removed from our database. Backups may keep copies for a short time before they are overwritten.

## 7. Children

Cookit! is a general-audience app and is not directed at young children. In the EU, you must be at least 15 (or the age set by your country) to create an account without a parent's permission. If you believe a child has given us personal information without permission, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encrypted connections, to protect your data. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If we make significant changes, we will tell you in the app.

## 10. Contact

Questions or requests: **cookit.app.support@protonmail.com**
